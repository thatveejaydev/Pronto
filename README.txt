PRONTO
Find project media faster in Premiere Pro.

PRONTO lets you search through project bins, preview audio, and place media at the timeline playhead from a small floating window.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
com.pronto.ppro - the complete plugin package and installer.
Terms of use.txt - draft usage terms for review before distribution.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows and Adobe Premiere Pro 2026.

INSTALLATION
1. Close Premiere Pro and extract the complete download.
2. Double-click Install PRONTO.bat in the main Pronto folder.
3. Wait for the success message.
4. Start Premiere Pro and open Window > Extensions > PRONTO Launcher.
5. Click Search project, or set an opening shortcut.

Administrator access is not required. The installer enables development extensions for your Windows account and installs to:
%APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\com.pronto.ppro

To update, close Premiere and run Install PRONTO.bat from the newer download.
Older PRONTO installations in known folders are moved out automatically to avoid duplicate copies.
Previous files are kept in %APPDATA%\Adobe\ProntoBackups.

UNINSTALLATION
Close Premiere Pro and double-click Uninstall PRONTO.bat.
It removes PRONTO from Adobe's extensions folder and keeps a recoverable backup.
Your projects, media, saved shortcuts, and other extensions are left alone.
Keep both batch files beside the com.pronto.ppro folder.

QUICK START
- Type a name or part of a bin path to search.
- Use Up and Down to choose a result.
- Click a result or press Enter to place it at the playhead.
- Enabled track targets get priority. PRONTO looks for enough room for the full clip.
- Shift+click or Shift+Enter opens media in the Source Monitor, or opens a sequence in its own timeline.
- Click the play button beside audio to preview it. Ctrl+Space is the initial preview shortcut.
- Press Esc to close the search window.
- Search results are remembered between openings and refreshed in the background about every 30 seconds.
- The first opening builds the list. Changing projects or restarting Premiere requires a new list.
- Press Ctrl+R to refresh the results immediately.

SHORTCUTS
Open PRONTO Launcher once each time you start Premiere.
Click a shortcut value, wait for Listening, then press and release your keys.
The shortcut saves automatically. Esc cancels. Right-click disables it.
Use different shortcuts for opening PRONTO and playing audio previews.

PLEASE NOTE
PRONTO is a prototype. Audio preview support depends on the media format.
It uses existing tracks and does not automatically create new ones.
Enter or click adds a sequence as a nested clip at the playhead. Click a Bin or press Enter to highlight it in the Project panel. Double-click the highlighted bin in Premiere to open its contents. A sequence cannot be nested inside itself.
Restart Premiere after updating the plugin.
