PRONTO helps you find and use media in your own Adobe Premiere Pro projects. You are responsible for having permission to use the media you work with.

2. Prototype software
PRONTO is provided as a prototype. Features may change, and compatibility is not guaranteed for every Premiere version, system, or media format. Keep backups of your projects and check edits made by the plugin.

3. Installation and shortcuts
The included installer enables development extensions for your Windows account. The Windows shortcut helper runs while the launcher is loaded. Choose shortcuts that do not interfere with your other applications.

4. Local media
PRONTO searches the open project and uses local media files for audio previews. Its current implementation does not upload your project or audio to an online service.
