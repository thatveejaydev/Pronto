Tips, Prefixes & Usage Guide

Search Prefixes

Type a prefix before your search terms to filter by media type:

  • a: or @audio or sfx: — Audio files
  • v: or @video — Video clips
  • i: or @image — Still photos & images
  • g: or @graphic — Graphics, MOGRTs & titles
  • s: or @seq — Sequences
  • b: or @bin — Project bins (folders)

Quick Filter Pills & Recent Items

  • Click any pill (All, Recent, Audio, Video, Image, Graphic, Seq, Bin) in the search window to filter results with one click. Click an active pill again to reset to All.
  • Press Down Arrow when the search box is empty (or click Recent) to browse your last 15 used items in the project.

Result Actions

  • Enter / Click — Places media or a nested sequence at the playhead in the first free track gap (or highlights a Bin in the Project panel).
  • Shift+Enter / Shift+Click — Opens media in the Source Monitor, or opens/switches to a Sequence tab in the Timeline.
  • Ctrl+Shift+Enter — Opens a Sequence in the Source Monitor.
  • Alt+Enter / Alt+Click — Reveals and highlights any item or bin inside Premiere's Project panel.
  • Ctrl+R — Rescans the active project immediately.
  • Esc — Stops audio preview and closes the search window.

Shortcuts & Audio Preview

  • Click Open PRONTO or Audio preview above and press your key combination (e.g. Alt+Space). Press Esc to cancel, or right-click a badge to disable it.
  • Enable Auto audio preview above to automatically audition sound effects as you arrow up and down through results.
  • Trimmed audio clips preview starting from their Premiere In point and stop at their Out point.
