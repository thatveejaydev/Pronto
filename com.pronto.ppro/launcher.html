Tips & keyboard shortcuts

Search Prefixes

Type a prefix before your search terms to filter by media type:

Search matches item names and bin paths. Use multiple words to narrow results; partial and fuzzy matches are supported (for example, a: swoosh).

  • a: or @audio or sfx: — Audio files
  • v: or @video — Video clips
  • i: or @image — Still photos & images
  • g: or @graphic — Graphics, MOGRTs & titles
  • s: or @seq — Sequences
  • b: or @bin — Project bins (folders)

Quick Filter Pills & Recent Items

  • Click any pill (All, Recent, Audio, Video, Image, Graphic, Seq, Bin) in the search window to filter results with one click. Click an active pill again to reset to All.
  • Tab — Next filter. Shift+Tab — Previous filter. Filters wrap around, apply immediately, and keep your search text ready for typing.
  • Up / Down Arrow — Select the previous or next result. Ctrl+F — Focus and select the search text.
  • Press Down Arrow when the search box is empty (or click Recent) to browse your last 15 used items in the project.
  • Recent items are saved per project. Frequently used items get a ranking boost. Up to 100 matching results are shown.

Placement Preview

  • The slim row below the search field previews placement for the selected result. Checking… means the destination is being calculated; SAFE means a collision-free destination was found; BLOCKED explains why placement is unavailable.
  • Blue V badges show video destinations; teal A badges show audio destinations. An audio range spans multiple tracks; A3+, for example, means the audio track count is unknown and space from A3 upward is checked conservatively.
  • The row also shows the start time and clip duration. Hover over it for the full status message. Bins do not show a placement preview.
  • Placement is checked again when you insert. The preview is an estimate of available space, not a reservation. Open a timeline and relink offline media before inserting; if no gap is available, add an empty destination track.

Result Actions

  • Enter / Click — Places media or a nested sequence at the playhead in the first free track gap (or highlights a Bin in the Project panel).
  • Shift+Enter / Shift+Click — Opens media in the Source Monitor, or opens/switches to a Sequence tab in the Timeline.
  • Ctrl+Shift+Enter — Opens a Sequence in the Source Monitor.
  • Alt+Enter / Alt+Click — Reveals and highlights any item or bin inside Premiere's Project panel.
  • Ctrl+R — Rescans the active project immediately.
  • Esc — Stops audio preview and closes the search window.

Shortcuts & Audio Preview

  • Click Open PRONTO or Audio preview above and press your key combination (e.g. Alt+Space). Press Esc to cancel, or right-click a badge to disable it.
  • Enable Auto-preview selected audio above to audition audio as you type or navigate results.
  • Use the configured Audio preview shortcut or the result's play button to play/pause audio. Moving to another result or leaving the search window stops playback.
  • Trimmed audio clips preview starting from their Premiere In point and stop at their Out point.
  • If a format cannot be previewed, press Shift+Enter to audition it in Premiere's Source Monitor.
  • The Open PRONTO shortcut works while Premiere is in the foreground. The blue Active in Premiere indicator means the shortcut helper reported it ready.
  • Click the Shortcuts or Audio heading to collapse its settings. Reopen this guide whenever you need the full keyboard reference.
